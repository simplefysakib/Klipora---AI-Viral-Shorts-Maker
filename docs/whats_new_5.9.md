# Klipora AI 5.9 — What’s New

## Universal Best Shorts Engine

Klipora AI now analyzes the full source timeline and prioritizes complete, independently understandable Shorts instead of random highlights. Each selected clip is evaluated for topic clarity, hook strength, context, useful information or entertainment value, payoff, natural pacing, and ending quality.

## Global Language Support

The AI workflow now detects the source video’s spoken language and writing system. English, Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu, Arabic, Bengali, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Nepali, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and other languages are supported through a language-neutral selection policy. Titles, hooks, summaries, and source metadata preserve the original language unless another output language is explicitly selected.

## Better Story Boundaries

Shorts now require a clear topic opening, necessary context, complete explanation or event, payoff, and natural ending. The editor avoids mid-sentence starts, unexplained replies, duplicate topics, incomplete answers, and endings before a punchline or result. Maximum duration is 60 seconds, with a preferred range of 20–55 seconds depending on the topic.

## YouTube Error Handling

Age-restricted YouTube videos are now identified immediately. Klipora no longer wastes time retrying alternate downloader clients for an authentication-only restriction and shows a clear message explaining that the source requires authorized login access. Network and format errors continue to use their normal recovery paths.
