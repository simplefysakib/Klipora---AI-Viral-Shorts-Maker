# Klipora AI 5.9.1 — What's New

Klipora AI 5.9.1 adds the Universal Best Shorts Engine. Instead of choosing random loud moments, the AI analyzes the full timestamped timeline and scores hook strength, topic clarity, context, value, retention, payoff, audio/visual quality, and natural pacing.

The new complete-story contract requires every Short to be understandable on its own: a clear hook, enough context, the main event or explanation, a result or payoff, and a natural ending. Shorts are capped at 60 seconds.

Global-language support now preserves the detected spoken language and writing system across titles, hooks, narration metadata, captions context, and source explanations. Non-English videos are not rejected or forced into English examples.

Age-restricted YouTube sources now stop retrying alternate download clients and show a clear authorized-login message. The app-version notification checker is permanently disabled in this release to prevent stale or incorrect update prompts.
